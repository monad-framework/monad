# Milestones

A milestone is a major, reviewable outcome that materially changes product
capability, evidence, or readiness. It may span multiple increments but must be
small enough to support a meaningful continue, correct, or stop decision.

## Naming

Use `MS-NNNN-short-outcome.md`. Numbers are chronological and never reused.
Titles describe the achieved condition, such as `Validated Primary Journey`,
not an activity such as `Build Features`.

## Milestone record

Each record contains:

- outcome statement and connection to vision goals;
- scope, exclusions, accountable owner, and target review window;
- entry evidence and assumptions;
- ordered increments and critical dependencies;
- outcome, quality, security, operational, and economic measures;
- risks, stop conditions, and contingency;
- exit evidence and approval authorities;
- final review decision and lessons.

## Lifecycle

Statuses are `proposed`, `authorized`, `active`, `at risk`, `achieved`,
`stopped`, and `superseded`. Only one status is current. Authorization confirms
that the outcome justifies investment and initial increments are feasible; it
does not commit every later scope detail.

## Review

At milestone review, compare actual outcomes with baselines and guardrails,
inspect unresolved risk and operational burden, and decide whether to proceed,
correct, narrow, expand, pause, or stop. Incomplete work is not automatically
carried forward. Preserve the review result in the milestone record and journal.
