# Milestones

A milestone is a major, reviewable outcome that materially changes product capability, evidence, or readiness. It may span one or more increments but must be small enough to support a meaningful continue, correct, re-scope, or stop decision.

## Active naming

Monad uses chronological `M-NNN` milestone IDs with descriptive filenames. IDs are never reused.

## Milestone record

Each record contains:

- outcome statement and connection to Product Goal/vision;
- scope, exclusions, accountable authority, and target review window;
- entry evidence and assumptions;
- ordered increments and critical dependencies;
- outcome, quality, security, privacy, operational, performance, provenance, and economic measures as applicable;
- risks, stop conditions, and contingency;
- exit evidence and approval/attestation authorities;
- final review decision and lessons.

## Lifecycle

Statuses include `proposed`, `authorized`, `active`, `at risk`, `achieved`, `stopped`, and `superseded`. Only one status is current. Authorization confirms that the outcome justifies investment and initial increments are feasible; it does not commit every later scope detail or authorize child Work Packets.

## Review

At milestone review, compare actual outcomes with baselines and guardrails, inspect unresolved risk, security/privacy posture, operational burden, cost, performance claims, compatibility/migration, audit/attestation evidence where applicable, and decide whether to proceed, correct, narrow, expand, pause, or stop. Incomplete work is not automatically carried forward. Preserve the review result in the milestone record and journal/evidence system.
