# Active Work Packets

This index contains only work explicitly authorized for current execution.
Detailed scope and acceptance live in the individual packet record. Work is
removed from this list when it is done, cancelled, superseded, or returned to
the backlog.

## Work-in-progress policy

- One active packet per accountable owner unless an expedite is approved.
- Finish review and integration before pulling another packet.
- Update blockers within one business day with a named decision or dependency.
- Do not add work directly; it must meet the Definition of Ready and belong to
  an authorized increment or inception outcome.

## Active

### WP-0001 — Validated problem and current-state baseline

- **Status:** active
- **Owner:** Product owner
- **Outcome:** representative evidence confirms, narrows, or rejects the core
  problem and establishes a quantitative baseline for the primary journey.
- **Acceptance:** at least five practitioner and three accountable-stakeholder
  interviews; at least three direct workflow or artifact observations; baseline
  definitions and measurements; alternatives compared; assumptions A-01 through
  A-05 updated; findings reviewed with product and engineering.
- **Dependencies:** access to representative participants and non-sensitive
  workflow evidence.
- **Evidence location:** `research/findings/` and approved updates to vision and
  product documents.

## Blocked

No packet is currently classified as blocked. When one is blocked, record the
start date, reason, impact, owner of the unblock action, and next escalation
time rather than using status alone.

## Recently completed

The documentation and governance scaffold was established during project
inception. Completion of a scaffold is not treated as validation of the product
hypothesis.
