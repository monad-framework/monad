## Decision

ADR-0008 — AI-Driven Engineering as the Default EOS Operating Model is **Accepted**.

The explicit human authority statement was:

> I accept ADR-0008.

## Effect

This decision makes ADR-0008 normative architectural authority, subject to higher-order constitutional, legal, security, and governance constraints.

The accepted architectural direction is:

- AI-driven, human-governed, evidence-closed engineering is the default Monad engineering operating model where suitable AI capability is available.
- AI may proactively drive analysis, context compilation, planning, clarification, recommendations, artifact generation, and bounded authorized work.
- AI initiative does not itself confer engineering authority.
- EOS remains Monad's sole engineering lifecycle and control plane.
- Humans retain accountable authority for consequential meaning, delegation, accepted risk, irreversible commitments, and final escalation.
- Acceptance remains governed by evidence, verification, review, and explicit authority rather than executor self-report.
- AI-assisted, AI-driven, and bounded AI-autonomous work are profiles of one operating model rather than competing lifecycle methodologies.
- Adaptive engineering pathways may vary rigor according to consequence, risk, complexity, uncertainty, reversibility, dependencies, evidence, and policy, but may not bypass mandatory EOS gates.
- Raw AI transcripts are noncanonical.
- AI providers, models, and harnesses remain replaceable and non-authoritative.
- Progressive autonomy must remain explicit, bounded, evidence-backed, observable, policy-controlled, and revocable.
- ADR-0006 remains authoritative and is not superseded.
- ADR-0007 remains independently governed and is not implicitly accepted by this decision.

## Non-effect

This acceptance does **not**:

- accept ADR-0007;
- authorize product-runtime implementation;
- create or authorize a new Work Cycle or Work Packet;
- broaden WP-MVP-0005;
- grant an AI executor additional repository, filesystem, process, network, production, or external-service privileges;
- create a ninth EOS lifecycle layer;
- introduce a second canonical state authority;
- authorize AI self-approval, self-acceptance, or gate bypass;
- make raw model output canonical engineering truth;
- close MNT-0003.
