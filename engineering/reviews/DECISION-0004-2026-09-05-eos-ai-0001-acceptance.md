# DECISION-0004-2026-09-05 — EOS-AI-0001 Acceptance

**Record type:** Governance authority decision
**Date:** 2026-09-05
**Subject:** EOS-AI-0001 — AI-Driven Engineering Operating Model
**Authority:** Human Project Steward / Architecture Owner
**Disposition:** **ACCEPTED**
**Related:** ADR-0006, ADR-0008, CR-0003, EOS-AI-0001, REV-EOS-AI-0001-0001

## Decision

`EOS-AI-0001 — AI-Driven Engineering Operating Model` is **Accepted**.

The explicit human authority statement was:

> I accept EOS-AI-0001.

## Effect

This decision makes `EOS-AI-0001` the governance-authoritative cross-layer operating-model baseline for AI participation in the Monad Engineering Operating System.

The accepted operating model establishes that:

* AI-driven, human-governed, evidence-closed engineering is the default operating model where suitable AI capability is available;
* AI may proactively inspect, reason, recommend, propose, plan, clarify, generate candidate artifacts, perform bounded authorized execution, assist verification and review, interpret evidence, and propose replanning;
* AI initiative does not itself confer engineering authority;
* EOS remains Monad's sole engineering lifecycle and control plane;
* the permanent EOS lifecycle remains EOSB, EOSP, EOSE, EOSV, EOSR, EOSC, EOSL, and EOSM;
* `.eos/state/current.json` remains the sole current EOS operational authority;
* human sovereignty remains grounded in accountability, delegated authority, accepted risk, mission, irreversible commitments, revocation, and escalation;
* adaptive engineering pathways may vary rigor but may not bypass mandatory EOS gates, required authority, policy, verification, review independence, or change control;
* material ambiguity must be surfaced rather than silently converted into governing engineering meaning;
* execution remains bounded by authorized scope, capability, environment, policy, and autonomy;
* executor completion remains a claim until required evidence, verification, review, and acceptance are satisfied;
* governing-input drift may invalidate or suspend stale execution authority;
* raw AI transcripts and private model reasoning are not canonical engineering state;
* provider, model, and harness identity do not confer semantic authority;
* AI-assisted, AI-driven, and bounded AI-autonomous operation are profiles within one operating model;
* progressive autonomy remains explicit, bounded, evidence-backed, observable, policy-controlled, attributable, and revocable;
* engineering and operational evidence may inform future reasoning while retaining its actual authority classification.

## Non-effect

This acceptance does **not**:

* accept ADR-0007;
* create a ninth EOS lifecycle layer;
* create a second canonical state authority;
* authorize unrestricted AI autonomy;
* authorize AI self-approval or self-acceptance;
* make model confidence or consensus authoritative;
* authorize product-runtime implementation;
* create or authorize implementation Work Cycles or Work Packets;
* broaden any currently authorized MVP Work Packet;
* close CR-0003;
* close MNT-0003.
