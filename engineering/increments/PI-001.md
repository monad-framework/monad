# PI-001: Architecture Baseline

**State:** DRAFT
**Phase:** Engineering Operating System Bootstrap

## Objective

Complete the architecture baseline, approve the foundational architectural decisions, establish the specification baseline, and exit the increment with an implementation-ready engineering system.

## Work Cycles

- WC-0001 — Architecture and Boundaries
- WC-0002 — Acceptance and Specification Baseline
- WC-0003 — Implementation Readiness

## Planned Work Packets

- WP-0001
- WP-0002
- WP-0003
- WP-0004

## Exit Conditions

PI-001 is complete only when:

- architecture is documented sufficiently to govern implementation;
- critical architectural decisions are accepted;
- critical quality attributes are defined;
- the initial specification baseline is accepted;
- at least one implementation Work Packet is ready;
- known blocking risks are controlled or explicitly accepted; and
- the bootstrap-to-execution transition is formally reviewed.
