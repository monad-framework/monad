## Feature readiness

A Feature is ready to supply Sprint Stories/Enablers when its outcome is clear, dependencies are known enough to sequence work, its governing product/architecture assumptions are explicit, and at least one thin vertical Story can demonstrate progress without requiring hidden authority.

Feature target dates are forecasts. Moving a Feature does not change accepted product/architecture meaning; changes to scope or authority follow the appropriate governance process.
