# Work Cycles

A work cycle is a short, fixed execution and learning window. It limits work in
progress, creates a frequent integration point, and provides evidence for
reforecasting. A cycle is not successful merely because every planned task was
started.

## Cycle protocol

### Plan

Confirm the increment objective, available capacity, carryover reason, and top
risks. Pull only ready packets. Sequence the smallest path to integrated
evidence and identify who can unblock external decisions.

### Execute

Keep active ownership and status visible. Integrate continuously, review early,
and swarm on blockers or nearly complete packets before starting more work.
Update acceptance evidence as it is produced.

### Review

Demonstrate completed outcomes and risk reduction in a representative context.
Record accepted packets, unmet evidence, defects, and product or architecture
findings. Do not count partial work as delivered value.

### Improve

Inspect flow time, blocked time, rework, escaped defects, review latency, and
unplanned work. Select at most one or two concrete process experiments for the
next cycle and assign owners.

## Suggested limits

Use no more concurrent packets than active contributors and aim for fewer.
Packets blocked beyond one business day require an explicit escalation or
re-plan. A packet spanning multiple cycles is split unless its indivisibility is
documented and approved.

## Cycle record

Name records `WC-NNNN-YYYY-MM-DD.md` and include objective, capacity, selected
packets, risk focus, completed evidence, metrics, decisions, carryover, and the
improvement experiment. Link it from the parent increment.
