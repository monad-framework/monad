## Sprint events

- **Planning:** select only Ready packets that support the Sprint Goal and fit demonstrated capacity.
- **Daily inspection:** reconcile completed/active/blocked/decision-required/next work; no status theater.
- **Refinement:** keep at least two future Sprints sufficiently decomposed.
- **Review:** demonstrate integrated evidence against the Sprint Goal.
- **Retrospective:** record one or more actionable process/system improvements.

## Capacity

Do not commit 100% of theoretical capacity. Target at most ~80% planned work to preserve review, debugging, evidence, discovery, and correction. Scope is the primary lever when the forecast slips; quality/security/determinism gates do not silently shrink.

## Release forecast

November 8, 2026 is an aggressive planning target, not a promise. If evidence requires more work, the release moves rather than relabeling incomplete behavior as MVP.
