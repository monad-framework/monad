## Sprint events

- **Planning:** select only Ready packets that support the Sprint Goal and fit demonstrated capacity.
- **Daily inspection:** reconcile completed/active/blocked/decision-required/next work.
- **Refinement:** maintain at least two future Work Cycles with bounded task/test/evidence decomposition.
- **Review:** demonstrate integrated evidence against the Sprint Goal.
- **Retrospective:** record actionable process/system improvements or an evidence-based no-change disposition.

## Capacity and autonomy

Do not commit 100% of theoretical capacity. Preserve explicit capacity for review, red-team verification, debugging, evidence, security/privacy analysis, and corrective work. Multi-agent parallelism does not multiply trusted capacity mechanically; demonstrated throughput and review quality determine future planning assumptions.

## Forecast rule

If MVP Release 1 moves, this schedule shifts with it unless CR/EOSP replanning explicitly changes sequencing. Quality, determinism, provenance, security, privacy, cryptographic, and human-authority gates do not silently shrink to preserve dates.
