# Architecture Decision Records

Architecture Decision Records preserve the context and consequences of significant choices. An ADR explains why an accepted option was reasonable at the time; it is not a retrospective claim that alternatives had no merit.

`architecture/decisions/` is the single canonical ADR location for Monad. The former top-level `adrs/` path is retired and must not be recreated.

## Naming and lifecycle

Use `ADR-NNNN-short-kebab-title.md` with monotonically increasing numbers. Statuses are `Proposed`, `Accepted`, `Rejected`, `Deprecated`, and `Superseded`.

Accepted records are immutable except for factual corrections, status transitions, and supersession links. A changed decision receives a new ADR that supersedes the old one.

## Process

1. Copy `ADR-0000-template.md` and assign the next number.
2. State the decision pressure, constraints, assumptions, and evidence before presenting options.
3. Compare at least two credible options, including continuing the current state when relevant.
4. Describe positive, negative, neutral, operational, security, compatibility, migration, and maintenance consequences.
5. Identify evidence required to validate uncertain claims.
6. Obtain reviews from affected owners and record the accountable decision authority.
7. Link implementation and validation work.
8. Update this index in the same change that accepts, rejects, deprecates, or supersedes an ADR.

## Current architectural anchor

ADR-0001 establishes the Monad Knowledge Engine as a foundational architectural subsystem and states that Monad capabilities consume, create, or transform engineering knowledge. It remains authoritative until explicitly superseded by a later accepted ADR.

## ADR quality test

A reader unfamiliar with the discussion should understand the problem, forces, chosen option, rejected options, trade-offs, and how success or failure will be recognized. Vague preferences, technology lists without drivers, and decisions that merely restate existing implementation are returned for revision.

## Machine projection

ADRs are canonical human-readable sources. Machine-readable companions under `machine/` are deterministic derivatives generated by `scripts/sync-machine-docs.py`; they must never be edited as an independent source of architectural authority.
