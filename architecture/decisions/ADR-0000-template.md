# ADR-NNNN: Concise decision title

- **Status:** proposed
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Decision owners:** Architecture owner and affected capability owners
- **Reviewers:** Security, operations, product, and domain owners as applicable
- **Related:** requirement IDs, quality scenarios, work packets, and prior ADRs
- **Supersedes:** none
- **Superseded by:** none

## Decision summary

State the selected option and its intended scope in one paragraph. A reader
should know what will be done without reading the evaluation first.

## Context

Describe the problem, current state, decision deadline, affected users and
systems, known constraints, and why an explicit decision is required now.
Separate observed facts from assumptions.

## Decision drivers

Rank the forces that distinguish the options, such as correctness, security,
recoverability, latency, scale, portability, team capability, delivery time,
total cost, and reversibility. Link measurable quality scenarios.

## Options considered

### Option A — Descriptive name

Explain the design, prerequisites, strengths, weaknesses, failure modes,
operational impact, security impact, and exit cost.

### Option B — Descriptive name

Evaluate against the same drivers and level of detail as Option A.

### Option C — Continue the current state

When credible, explain the cost and risk of deferring or making no change.

## Decision

Identify the chosen option, why it best fits the ranked drivers, and which
disadvantages are knowingly accepted. State scope boundaries and any decisions
explicitly deferred.

## Consequences

### Positive

- List new capabilities, reduced risks, or simplified responsibilities.

### Negative

- List costs, constraints, coupling, migration burden, and new failure modes.

### Neutral or follow-on

- List training, documentation, monitoring, ownership, or future review needs.

## Security, privacy, and operations

Describe trust-boundary changes, data handling, privileges, telemetry, capacity,
deployment, rollback, backup, recovery, incident response, and on-call burden.

## Migration and rollback

Define phases, compatibility, data movement, cutover, verification, rollback
criteria, irreversible steps, and the owner of each transition.

## Validation

List experiments, tests, service indicators, cost measurements, and review dates
that will confirm or challenge the decision. State the condition that would
trigger reconsideration.

## References

Link primary documentation, internal evidence, research findings, prototypes,
and related decisions. Record access dates for changeable external sources.
