# Research System

Research reduces decision uncertainty with reproducible evidence. It is not a
collection of links or a mechanism for defending a preferred solution.

## Directory model

- `questions.md` — prioritized questions tied to pending decisions.
- `findings/` — synthesized evidence and decision implications.
- `experiments/` — protocols, observations, data, and reproducible results.
- `references/` — source notes, bibliographic records, and retrieval metadata.

## Research workflow

1. Name the decision and the uncertainty that prevents it.
2. State a falsifiable question and current hypothesis.
3. Select the least costly method that can produce decision-grade evidence.
4. Define population, sample, procedure, measures, consent, and data handling.
5. Collect observations without changing success criteria after seeing results.
6. Separate observations, interpretation, limitations, and recommendations.
7. Review the finding with an affected owner and link the resulting decision.
8. Retain or delete research data according to its classification and purpose.

## Evidence hierarchy

Direct observation of representative behavior normally outweighs stated future
preference. Production or pilot outcome data outweighs demonstration success.
Primary technical documentation and reproducible tests outweigh unsourced
summaries. Multiple independent evidence types increase confidence; volume of
weak evidence does not.

## Ethics and privacy

Collect only what the question requires, explain participation and use, avoid
coercion, and protect identities in synthesis. Research involving sensitive
topics, vulnerable groups, covert observation, or high-impact decisions requires
appropriate expert and ethical review before recruitment or collection.

## Finding standard

Name findings `FND-NNNN-short-claim.md` and include question, method, sample,
observations, analysis, confidence, limitations, competing explanations,
decision impact, and source links. A finding can be `supported`, `mixed`,
`unsupported`, or `inconclusive`; it is never upgraded by optimism.
